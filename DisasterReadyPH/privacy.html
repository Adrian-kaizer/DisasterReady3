Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

DisasterReady PH is a student project prototype. This policy describes what information the app handles and how it is used. The app is not an official government emergency service. When run locally, the host computer stores account information in its Neon PostgreSQL database. When deployed to Netlify, account information is stored in the project's configured Neon PostgreSQL database.

Information stored by the app

  • Account information: your email address and a salted password hash are stored in the configured app database (hosted Neon PostgreSQL). Your plain-text password is not stored.
  • Profile picture: if you upload one, the image and its format are stored with your account.
  • Session information: when you sign in, the app uses an HTTP-only session cookie and a server-side session record to keep you signed in. Sessions expire after eight hours.
  • Preparedness and safety information: checklist progress is saved to your account. Hazard reports are stored in the database and are visible to the account that submitted them. Flood-monitoring stations and readings are shared across signed-in users; administrator accounts manage station data. Reports are not automatically sent to emergency responders.

Location, weather, and map services

Weather location searches use Open-Meteo's geocoding service and request weather forecasts using the selected coordinates. Map searches are sent through this app's server to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service, and map tiles are provided by OpenStreetMap. These services receive the requests needed to provide their features, including the location search or coordinates requested. Open-Meteo and Nominatim see this app server's network address for proxied requests. OpenStreetMap receives tile requests from your browser, including its network address.

If you choose a GPS location, your browser asks permission to share your device location with this page. The coordinates are used to position the map and may be used for local weather. They are saved to your account only if you explicitly choose to save a personal map pin.

The app loads the Leaflet map library from unpkg. These external providers have their own privacy practices and terms.

How information is used and retained

Account and profile information is used to provide sign-in and account features. It remains in the app database until you delete your account using Account settings. Deleting your account removes the account and its profile picture. Browser-only information remains on your device until you clear this site's browser data.

The app does not sell your information or use it for advertising. The prototype does not provide a way to send reports to emergency services.

Security and your choices

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, and session cookies are HTTP-only. Do not use a password you rely on for another service. You can remove your account from Account settings. Netlify deployments should use HTTPS and keep the Neon connection string in Netlify's private environment-variable settings.

Children and sensitive information

Do not enter sensitive personal information in hazard reports or other free-text fields. The prototype is not designed to collect information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the prototype changes. The project currently does not publish a dedicated privacy contact address.
